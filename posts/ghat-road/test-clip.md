# Ghat road — 8-second test clip

**Purpose:** test the camera-journey format ([../../playbook/camera-journey-format.md](../../playbook/camera-journey-format.md))
before planning the whole post. This clip is written to also work as the real post's clip 1 (the hook).

**Tool:** Higgsfield, text-to-video, 9:16, 10 s, with the narration and SFX generated in the clip, in
the clean stylized 3D look. The first try in Google Flow (8 s, photoreal) failed on 17 Nov 2026. The
shot table below is the 8 s version; the Higgsfield prompt has the same beats spread over 10 s.

## The idea in one line
Why don't mountain roads go straight up? The camera falls from the sky straight onto a hairpin bend,
lands **on the road surface**, and a loaded truck drives **right over the camera**. No drone or crew
can get that shot.

## Shot by shot (one continuous camera move, no cuts)
| Time | What we see | Narration |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| 0.0 → 0.5 s | Looking straight down from high in the sky: a green mountain, with a grey road coiled down it in tight hairpin bends. A small colourful truck is crawling up. Still. | — |
| 0.5 → 3.5 s | The camera starts falling straight down toward one hairpin bend, faster and faster, as the bend grows to fill the frame. | पहाड़ पर सड़क सीधी ऊपर क्यों नहीं जाती? |
| 3.5 → 6.5 s | Right above the tarmac it swoops level and lies flat on the road, looking up the slope. The truck comes round the bend and drives **straight over the camera**: big tyres roll past on both sides, and the dark underside and axle pass just overhead. | जवाब… इस truck के पहियों में छुपा है। |
| 6.5 → 8.0 s | The truck has passed. The camera holds still at road level, watching its back tyres grind slowly away up the steep road. | — |

Why it works as a hook: it asks a question everyone has wondered about on a hill trip, and it doesn't
answer it yet. The next clip pays it off: on a straight road up the mountain, the truck can't climb.

## Short prompt, 871 characters (17 Nov 2026)
Asked for a prompt of about 900 characters. This one keeps only the truck driving over the camera,
the most "impossible" move, so each clip has one move. The fall from the sky would be its own clip.

```text
Vertical 9:16, 8 s, clean stylized 3D animation, polished like a miniature diorama, smooth matte materials, not photoreal. One continuous shot, no cuts.
A green rounded mountain with simple soft trees; a clean grey road climbs it in tight hairpin bends with a low cream stone wall. Pastel blue sky, soft sunlight.
[0-2s] The camera lies flat on the road in the middle of a hairpin bend, lens just above the tarmac, looking uphill. An orange Indian goods truck with a blue cargo body and chunky black tyres rounds the bend toward the camera.
[2-5s] The truck drives straight over the camera: tyres roll past on both sides, its dark underside passes just overhead.
[5-8s] Sky opens again; camera holds still as the truck climbs away uphill.
A Hindi male narrator says once: "पहाड़ पर सड़क सीधी ऊपर क्यों नहीं जाती?"
Diesel rumble, tyre crunch. No music, no text, no people.
```

## How to judge the take
- **The fall:** one smooth move from the sky down to the road, with no jump cut in the middle.
- **The pass-over:** the truck really drives over the camera, with tyres on both sides. It mustn't
  crash into the camera or melt through it.
- **The truck** keeps the same shape and colours from start to end.
- **The voice:** both Hindi lines are clear, said once, with no extra words.
- **No text** in the frame.

## If it fails
| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| The fall and the pass-over don't both fit in 8 s, or it cuts | Split it into two clips: (A) the fall, 0–4 s; (B) a camera already lying on the road as the truck drives over it. Join them in CapCut on the moment the camera touches the road. |
| The truck hits or swallows the camera | Change "drives straight over the camera" to "drives past just beside the camera, its huge front tyre rolling by a hand's width away". |
| The Hindi comes out wrong | Write both lines in Roman letters: "Pahaad par sadak seedhi upar kyon nahin jaati?" / "Jawaab... is truck ke pahiyon mein chhupa hai." |
| It looks like real drone footage, not 3D | Keep it if it looks great. If not, add "clearly a polished 3D render" after "photoreal-leaning". |

## Mechanism check (for the full post, to verify before scripting)
Hill roads zigzag to keep the slope gentle enough for loaded trucks to climb and brake safely, and
hairpins are widened so long vehicles can turn. India's hill-road rules (IRC) set slope limits.
Look up the actual numbers before the full script.
