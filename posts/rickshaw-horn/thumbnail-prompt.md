# Thumbnail prompt — "रिक्शा का पों-पों हॉर्न बिना बिजली कैसे बजता है?"

Made 21 Nov 2026 (v2): a context prompt for ChatGPT with a Hindi (Devanagari) headline. Method:
[../../playbook/thumbnail.md](../../playbook/thumbnail.md). Paste it into a new ChatGPT chat and save the
result as `thumbnail.png`.

v2 changes, after Anand's feedback on v1: clear safe margins on every side, bolder and more attractive colours
(turquoise and pink street bokeh behind the gold horn), and a realistic, premium-render look in place of the
plain studio gradient.

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts thumbnail (1080×1920) for my Hindi science channel "Why Bhai", which explains how everyday things work in 3D.

VIDEO TOPIC: "रिक्शा का पों-पों हॉर्न बिना बिजली कैसे बजता है?" (How does the rickshaw's "pon-pon" horn sound with no electricity?)
What the video shows: the brass bulb horn on an Indian cycle-rickshaw has no battery and no wires, yet it goes "pon-pon". The black rubber bulb at the back is really a small air pump. Squeezing it pushes air fast into the brass neck, where a thin metal reed sits over a slot. The air pushes the reed open, it springs back, hundreds of times a second, and that shaking is the sound. The flared brass bell doesn't make the sound; it only makes it louder. The twist at the end: the horn's "battery" is the rickshaw driver's hand.
Visuals in the video: a colourful Indian street with a parked cycle-rickshaw and the brass horn on its handlebar; sound rings bursting from the bell; the black rubber bulb close up; the bulb cut open, hollow and full of mint-green air; the neck cut open to show the thin brass reed over its slot; the reed blurring as it vibrates; small cream sound rings growing large out of the bell; the horn next to an empty see-through battery shell.

WHAT TO MAKE: one eye-catching thumbnail that makes people curious enough to watch. It shouldn't give away the final answer. Pick the strongest idea from the video for the main visual. Write a short, catchy headline in Hindi (Devanagari script), 2 lines and 6 words at most.

DESIGN STYLE:
- It must look real: a high-end, realistic 3D render with the quality of premium product photography. Real polished brass with fine scratches and warm reflections, real matte black rubber with a subtle texture, a real black steel handlebar and clamp, true-to-life shadows and depth of field. Not a cartoon, not flat, not plastic or toy-like.
- Background: the colourful Indian street from the video, softly blurred into rich bokeh, mostly turquoise and pink painted walls with a few warm marigold-orange highlights. Keep yellow and gold out of the area right behind the horn, so the gold brass pops.
- Colours: bold, rich and attractive, with strong contrast between the warm gold horn and the cool turquoise behind it. Warm golden morning sunlight from one side. Sound shown as soft cream-white 3D rings with a thin coral edge. No neon, no glow effects, no dark blue/purple AI-style look, nothing washed out or muddy.
- One main subject, big and clear, that reads instantly at small phone size.
- Hindi text: big, bold, rounded, white with a thick dark outline and a soft shadow, placed in the upper third. One key word may be in warm yellow for punch. Keep the Devanagari spelling correct.
- Spacing: leave clear margins on every side. Keep the headline and the subject at least 8% of the width away from the left and right edges and at least 10% of the height below the top edge, so nothing touches or gets cut by any edge. Give the headline breathing room from the subject too. Keep the bottom quarter fairly empty, since the YouTube Shorts buttons cover it.
- No English text, logos, watermarks, people, hands or extra clutter.
```
