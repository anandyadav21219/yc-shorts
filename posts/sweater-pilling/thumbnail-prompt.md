# Thumbnail prompt — "स्वेटर पर गोलियाँ क्यों बनती हैं?"

Version 3 (19 Nov 2026): a context prompt for ChatGPT with a Hindi (Devanagari) headline. Anand confirmed it
works. Version 4 spelled out the rain thumbnail's badges and spacing and came out worse, so it was dropped.
Method: [../../playbook/thumbnail.md](../../playbook/thumbnail.md). Paste it into a new ChatGPT chat and save the
result as `images/thumbnail.png`.

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts thumbnail (1080×1920) for my Hindi science channel "Why Bhai", which explains how everyday things work in 3D.

VIDEO TOPIC: "स्वेटर पर गोलियाँ क्यों बनती हैं?" (Why do little balls, called pills, form on a sweater?)
What the video shows: the pills on a sweater don't come from outside. They are made of the sweater itself. Every yarn is made of short fibres twisted together, and their loose ends stick out. Rubbing under the arms and at the wrists pulls those ends out into fuzz, and the fuzz tangles and twists into a tight little ball: a pill. The twist at the end: the pill doesn't fall off because its fibres are too strong to break, so a pill is a sign of too much strength, not weakness.
Visuals in the video: a deep bottle-green knitted sweater on an invisible mannequin, extreme close-ups of the yarn with short fibres twisted in a spiral and loose ends standing up like hairs, the sleeve rubbing against the side of the sweater, fuzz spiralling into a ball, and a pill being pulled up while its fibres stretch tight and hold on.

WHAT TO MAKE: one eye-catching thumbnail that makes people curious enough to watch. It shouldn't give away the final answer. Pick the strongest idea from the video for the main visual. Write a short, catchy headline in Hindi (Devanagari script), 2 lines and 6 words at most.

DESIGN STYLE:
- Clean, premium stylized 3D, like a high-end motion-graphics studio. Not a real photo, not a cartoon.
- Smooth soft studio gradient background in warm, tasteful colours (cream, soft peach, soft yellow), with no real environment. No green or mint background, so the green sweater stands out.
- Soft matte wool with visible twisted yarn and fine fuzz. The pill is the same green as the sweater: a dense little ball with short fuzz, like a real sweater pill, not moss or a pompom.
- Soft studio lighting with gentle shadows.
- Accents in matte soft coral only. No neon, no glow effects, no dark blue/purple AI-style look.
- One main subject, big and clear, that reads instantly at small phone size.
- Hindi text: bold, rounded, white with a dark outline and a soft shadow, placed in the upper third. Keep the Devanagari spelling correct.
- Keep the bottom quarter fairly empty, since the YouTube Shorts buttons cover it.
- No English text, logos, watermarks, people, hands or extra clutter.
```
