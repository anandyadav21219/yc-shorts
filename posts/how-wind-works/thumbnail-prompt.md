# Thumbnail prompt — "हवा कैसे चलती है?"

Made 19 Nov 2026: a context prompt for ChatGPT with a Hindi (Devanagari) headline. Method:
[../../playbook/thumbnail.md](../../playbook/thumbnail.md). Paste it into a new ChatGPT chat and save the
result as `images/thumbnail.png`.

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts thumbnail (1080×1920) for my Hindi science channel "Why Bhai", which explains how everyday things work in 3D.

VIDEO TOPIC: "हवा कैसे चलती है?" (How does wind blow?)
What the video shows: no giant fan pushes the wind. The sun does it. By day the sun heats the land faster than the sea. The warm air over the land gets lighter and rises, so the pressure over the land drops. The cool, heavy air over the sea rushes in to fill the gap, and that rush is the wind (a sea breeze). The twist at the end: at night the whole loop runs the other way.
Visuals in the video: a small floating cut-away island, half sea and half land, with palm trees, small pastel houses, a striped flag and a little sailboat; a giant pink table fan crossed out; the sun heating the land with heat squiggles; two thermometers, hot over the land and cool over the sea; warm orange air bubbles rising over the land with coral up-arrows; two pressure gauges, low over the land and high over the sea; cool blue air bubbles rushing from the sea to the land with a big arrow, the flag streaming and the boat sailing; and the whole loop of moving air drawn as a colourful wind ribbon circling over the island.

WHAT TO MAKE: one eye-catching thumbnail that makes people curious enough to watch. It shouldn't give away the final answer. Pick the strongest idea from the video for the main visual. Write a short, catchy headline in Hindi (Devanagari script), 2 lines and 6 words at most.

DESIGN STYLE:
- Clean, premium stylized 3D, like a high-end motion-graphics studio, bright and a little playful. Not a real photo, not a cartoon.
- Smooth soft studio gradient background in tasteful colours (soft sky blue and warm peach), with no real environment.
- Soft rounded shapes: a calm glassy sea, soft green land with palm trees and pastel houses, a smooth sun, air shown as small soft bubbles (warm orange over the land, cool blue over the sea). Soft studio lighting with gentle shadows.
- Accents in matte soft coral (warm rising air) and soft blue (cool air) only. No neon, no glow effects, no dark blue/purple AI-style look.
- One main subject, big and clear, that reads instantly at small phone size.
- Hindi text: bold, rounded, white with a dark outline and a soft shadow, placed in the upper third. Keep the Devanagari spelling correct.
- Keep the bottom quarter fairly empty, since the YouTube Shorts buttons cover it.
- No English text, logos, watermarks, people, hands or extra clutter.
```
