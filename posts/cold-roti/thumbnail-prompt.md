```text
Create a vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts thumbnail (1080×1920) for my Hindi science channel "Why Bhai", which explains how everyday things work in 3D.

VIDEO TOPIC: "ठंडी रोटी सख़्त क्यों?" (Why does a cold roti go stiff?)
What the video shows: a hot roti is soft and bendy, but once it cools it doesn't only dry out, it stiffens from inside. Wheat flour is full of tiny starch grains. On the hot tawa they soak up water, swell and open up into loose threads, which keeps the roti soft. As the roti cools, those threads slowly pack back together into tight bundles, so the roti turns stiff and cracks when you bend it. The twist at the end: keeping rotis in the fridge makes them go stiff even faster, and the tawa's heat is what makes them soft again.
Visuals in the video: a golden phulka with brown spots puffed up and steaming on a black iron tawa over a blue gas flame on a steel stove; the flame turning off and the roti going flat; a cold roti on a steel thali that bends stiffly and cracks across; a hot roti folding softly in half with steam; the hot folded roti and the cold cracked roti side by side on two steel plates; inside the roti, cream-coloured starch grains, little blue water drops flowing into them, the grains swelling and loose white threads coming out; the light turning from warm orange to cool blue as the threads pack into tight bundles; a steel dabba of rotis on a fridge shelf in cold blue light; and the roti puffing up again on the tawa.

WHAT TO MAKE: one eye-catching thumbnail that makes people curious enough to watch. It shouldn't give away the final answer. Pick the strongest idea from the video for the main visual. Write a short, catchy headline in Hindi (Devanagari script), 2 lines and 6 words at most.

DESIGN STYLE:
- Clean, premium stylized 3D, like a high-end motion-graphics studio, bright and a little playful. Not a real photo, not a cartoon.
- Smooth soft studio gradient background in warm, tasteful colours (warm coral-orange for hot and soft sky blue for cold), with no kitchen or real environment. No beige or yellow background, so the golden roti stands out.
- A realistic golden-beige roti with brown spots and a soft matte surface, a matte black iron tawa, polished steel plates, soft white steam. Soft studio lighting with gentle shadows.
- Accents in matte soft coral (heat) and soft blue/mint (cold) only. No neon, no glow effects, no dark blue/purple AI-style look.
- One main subject, big and clear, that reads instantly at small phone size.
- Hindi text: bold, rounded, white with a dark outline and a soft shadow, placed in the upper third. Keep the Devanagari spelling correct.
- Keep the bottom quarter fairly empty, since the YouTube Shorts buttons cover it.
- No English text, logos, watermarks, people, hands or extra clutter.
```
