## Cover
On **Edit cover**, pick the frame at **about 4.6 s** (the pen writing "सुनीता" on the steel glass,
sparks at the tip), or use the ChatGPT thumbnail once it's made.

**Grid crop:** the glass, the pen and the name sit between about 250 and 1100 px, and the caption line at
about 1180 px. Both are inside the middle 3:4 the profile grid shows (240–1680 px), so the default crop works.
