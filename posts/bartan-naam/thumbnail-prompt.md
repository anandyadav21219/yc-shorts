# Thumbnail prompt — "बर्तन वाले भैया स्टील पर नाम कैसे लिख देते हैं?"

Made 20 Nov 2026: a context prompt for ChatGPT with a Hindi (Devanagari) headline. Method:
[../../playbook/thumbnail.md](../../playbook/thumbnail.md). Paste it into a new ChatGPT chat and save the
result as `images/thumbnail.png`.

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts thumbnail (1080×1920) for my Hindi science channel "Why Bhai", which explains how everyday things work in 3D.

VIDEO TOPIC: "बर्तन वाले भैया स्टील पर नाम कैसे लिख देते हैं?" (How does the utensil-shop man write your name on steel?)
What the video shows: the shop's engraving pen doesn't use ink at all. A small box with a transformer inside turns the home's mains power into just a few volts. A crocodile clip on the utensil makes the utensil itself part of the circuit. Every time the pen's thin metal tip touches the steel, a tiny spark jumps and melts a very small spot, which then cools into a dark dot. Hundreds of these dots, one after another, form the name, and because the mark is in the metal itself, it never washes off. The twist at the end: the name on your dabba is really hundreds of tiny marks left by electric sparks.
Visuals in the video: a polished stainless-steel glass on a utensil-shop shelf of thalis, glasses, a kadhai and a dabba; an engraving pen with a thin silver tip and a coiled cable that moves by itself; a small yellow metal machine box with a red switch, a knob and a pilot lamp; a red crocodile clip biting the glass rim; an Indian 3-pin plug going into a white switchboard; a cutaway of the box showing the transformer's iron core and copper coils; a glowing current loop running from the box through the pen, the glass and the clip; a macro of brushed steel where a blue-white spark flashes and a tiny spot glows orange, then cools to a dark dot; the name "सुनीता" made of dark dots on the glass; a check cotton cloth rubbing the name, which stays; the same name on a steel dabba.

WHAT TO MAKE: one eye-catching thumbnail that makes people curious enough to watch. It shouldn't give away the final answer. Pick the strongest idea from the video for the main visual. Write a short, catchy headline in Hindi (Devanagari script), 2 lines and 6 words at most.

DESIGN STYLE:
- Clean, premium stylized 3D, like a high-end motion-graphics studio, bright and a little playful. Not a real photo, not a cartoon.
- Smooth soft studio gradient background in warm, tasteful colours (saffron, coral and soft peach), with no shop interior or real environment. No grey or silver background, so the steel glass stands out.
- Mirror-polished stainless steel with crisp studio reflections; the name as a line of small dark dots in the metal; a black pen with a thin silver tungsten tip; a small, crisp blue-white spark with a few hot orange sparkles at the tip as the only bright light. Soft studio lighting with gentle shadows.
- Accents in matte soft coral only. No neon, no glow effects, no dark blue/purple AI-style look.
- One main subject, big and clear, that reads instantly at small phone size.
- Hindi text: bold, rounded, white with a dark outline and a soft shadow, placed in the upper third. Keep the Devanagari spelling correct.
- Keep the bottom quarter fairly empty, since the YouTube Shorts buttons cover it.
- No English text, logos, watermarks, people, hands or extra clutter.
```
