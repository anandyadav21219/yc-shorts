# Thumbnail prompt — "पंखे का रेगुलेटर स्पीड कैसे घटाता-बढ़ाता है?"

Made 20 Nov 2026: a context prompt for ChatGPT with a Hindi (Devanagari) headline. Method:
[../../playbook/thumbnail.md](../../playbook/thumbnail.md). Paste it into a new ChatGPT chat and save the
result as `images/thumbnail.png`.

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts thumbnail (1080×1920) for my Hindi science channel "Why Bhai", which explains how everyday things work in 3D.

VIDEO TOPIC: "पंखे का रेगुलेटर स्पीड कैसे घटाता-बढ़ाता है?" (How does a fan regulator make the fan slower or faster?)
What the video shows: the small knob on the wall controls how much electricity reaches the ceiling fan. The old black regulator had a coil of wire inside; it let less electricity through to the fan and burnt the rest off as heat, which is why it got warm. The new white regulator has capacitors instead of a coil: they don't burn electricity, they hold it and hand it back, and turning the knob connects a smaller or bigger capacitor, which changes the speed. The twist at the end: on speed 1 the new regulator really saves electricity, while the old one wasted that same electricity as heat.
Visuals in the video: a white modular switch plate with a round regulator knob, a white three-blade ceiling fan seen from below (fast, then slow), an old black bakelite dome regulator on a wooden board, its cut-away inside with a silvery wire coil and brass contacts, warm heat haze with coral arrows rising off the coil, the new regulator cut open with blue-grey box capacitors and a rotary switch, golden-yellow energy arrows, and the old hot regulator beside the new cool one.

WHAT TO MAKE: one eye-catching thumbnail that makes people curious enough to watch. It shouldn't give away the final answer. Pick the strongest idea from the video for the main visual. Write a short, catchy headline in Hindi (Devanagari script), 2 lines and 6 words at most.

DESIGN STYLE:
- Clean, premium stylized 3D, like a high-end motion-graphics studio. Not a real photo, not a cartoon.
- Smooth soft studio gradient background in warm, tasteful colours (peach, coral pink, cream or soft mint), with no room or real environment.
- Glossy white plastic, black bakelite, warm varnished wood, brass and silvery wire; soft studio lighting with gentle shadows.
- Accents in matte golden-yellow (electricity) and soft coral (heat) only. No neon, no glow effects, no dark blue/purple AI-style look.
- One main subject, big and clear, that reads instantly at small phone size.
- Hindi text: bold, rounded, white with a dark outline and a soft shadow, placed in the upper third. Keep the Devanagari spelling correct.
- Keep the bottom quarter fairly empty, since the YouTube Shorts buttons cover it.
- No English text, logos, watermarks, people, hands or extra clutter.
```
