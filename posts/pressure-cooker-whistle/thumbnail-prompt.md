`images/thumbnail.png`. Method: [../../playbook/thumbnail.md](../../playbook/thumbnail.md). Paste it into a new
ChatGPT chat and save the result as `images/thumbnail.png`.

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts thumbnail (1080×1920) for my Hindi science channel "Why Bhai", which explains how everyday things work in 3D.

VIDEO TOPIC: "कुकर सीटी क्यों बजाता है?" (Why does a pressure cooker whistle?)
What the video shows: inside a closed pressure cooker, water boils into steam. The sealed lid traps the steam, so the pressure inside keeps rising, and that's why food cooks in about half the time. The small weight on top of the lid works like a gatekeeper: when the pressure gets strong enough, it lifts the weight a little, a burst of steam rushes out, and that's the whistle. The twist at the end: the cooker isn't really whistling, it's breathing out at exactly the right time.
Visuals in the video: a polished stainless-steel Indian pressure cooker with black handles, a domed lid and a weighted whistle on top, on a single-burner gas stove with a ring of blue flame; a cut-away of the cooker showing water boiling into white steam inside, with dal and vegetables cooking at the bottom; coral arrows showing the steam pressure pushing on the walls and pushing up under the weight; and the weight lifting as a jet of white steam escapes from the vent.

WHAT TO MAKE: one eye-catching thumbnail that makes people curious enough to watch. It shouldn't give away the final answer. Pick the strongest idea from the video for the main visual. Write a short, catchy headline in Hindi (Devanagari script), 2 lines and 6 words at most.

DESIGN STYLE:
- Clean, premium stylized 3D, like a high-end motion-graphics studio. Not a real photo, not a cartoon.
- Smooth soft studio gradient background in warm, tasteful colours (soft yellow, peach, coral pink, cream, a hint of mint), with no kitchen or real environment.
- Brushed stainless steel and black handles with controlled reflections; soft semi-transparent white steam; a clean blue gas flame; soft studio lighting with gentle shadows.
- Accents in matte soft coral only, for the pressure. No neon, no glow effects, no dark blue/purple AI-style look.
- One main subject, big and clear, that reads instantly at small phone size.
- Hindi text: bold, rounded, white with a dark outline and a soft shadow, placed in the upper third. Keep the Devanagari spelling correct.
- Keep the bottom quarter fairly empty, since the YouTube Shorts buttons cover it.
- No English text, logos, watermarks, people, hands or extra clutter.
```
