# Thumbnail prompt — "बारिश कैसे होती है?"

Made 19 Nov 2026: a context prompt for ChatGPT with a Hindi (Devanagari) headline. Method:
[../../playbook/thumbnail.md](../../playbook/thumbnail.md). Paste it into a new ChatGPT chat and save the
result as `images/thumbnail.png`. When you copy this folder for a new Short, rewrite the prompt from the new
post's README.

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts thumbnail (1080×1920) for my Hindi science channel "Why Bhai", which explains how everyday things work in 3D.

VIDEO TOPIC: "बारिश कैसे होती है?" (How does rain happen?)
What the video shows: the sun heats the sea, and the water rises as soft white vapour. High up in the cold, the vapour turns into tiny droplets that form a cloud. The droplets merge into bigger, heavier drops until the rising air can't hold them up, and they fall as rain. The twist at the end: a raindrop is not tear-shaped like everyone draws it. It's shaped like a pav (round on top, flat at the bottom).
Visuals in the video: a small floating cut-away island (half sea, half land with trees and soil layers), the sun, a white cloud turning grey and raining, glass-like water drops merging, coral and mint arrows, and a tear-shaped drop being proven wrong.

WHAT TO MAKE: one eye-catching thumbnail that makes people curious enough to watch. It shouldn't give away the final answer. Pick the strongest idea from the video for the main visual. Write a short, catchy headline in Hindi (Devanagari script), 2 lines and 6 words at most.

DESIGN STYLE:
- Clean, premium stylized 3D, like a high-end motion-graphics studio. Not a real photo, not a cartoon.
- Smooth soft studio gradient background in warm, tasteful colours (peach, cream, soft mint or soft sky blue), with no real environment.
- Glossy, glass-like water; soft fluffy clouds; soft studio lighting with gentle shadows.
- Accents in matte soft coral and mint only. No neon, no glow effects, no dark blue/purple AI-style look.
- One main subject, big and clear, that reads instantly at small phone size.
- Hindi text: bold, rounded, white with a dark outline and a soft shadow, placed in the upper third. Keep the Devanagari spelling correct.
- Keep the bottom quarter fairly empty, since the YouTube Shorts buttons cover it.
- No English text, logos, watermarks, people or extra clutter.
```
