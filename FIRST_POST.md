**How a pressure cooker whistle works**

## Design (this post)

**Object & materials:**
- Steel Indian pressure cooker, brushed stainless body and lid — `#B8C2C8`.
- Weighted whistle regulator on the vent pipe; rubber safety valve on the lid.
- Scene colours: warm kitchen ambient `#E8B878`, gas flame `#247BFF`.
- Generate one master cooker image first and reuse it across all four clips.

**Per-shot camera:**
1. **Exterior hook** — slow push-in, three-quarter angle; regulator jiggles, steam escapes; short macro close-up.
2. **Pressure builds** — camera transitions *through* the lid into the cutaway; boiling water, rising steam, orange pressure arrows on the chamber walls.
3. **Whistle mechanism** — extreme close-up: the weighted regulator lifts off the vent pipe, a steam jet releases, then it settles back.
4. **Safety + loop** — highlight the safety release as a backup, then return to the opening exterior framing for a seamless loop.

**Accuracy note:** the whistle is steam escaping the **weighted regulator**; the **safety
valve is a backup** and must not open during normal cooking — show it only as an exceptional
condition.

## Clip 1 — Hook (0–11s)
- **स्क्रीन टेक्स्ट:** *कुकर सीटी क्यों बजाता है?*
- **Voiceover:** "हर भारतीय किचन में ये आवाज़ आती है। लेकिन कुकर आख़िर सीटी बजाता क्यों है? चलिए, अंदर चलते हैं।"
- **Flow prompt:** *3D render, 9:16 vertical. A steel Indian pressure cooker on a gas stove, blue flame lit. The weight on top jiggles and a sharp burst of steam shoots up with a whistle. Camera slowly pushes in toward the lid. Warm kitchen lighting, shallow depth of field, photoreal.*

## Clip 2 — प्रेशर बनता है (11–22s)
- **स्क्रीन टेक्स्ट:** *भाप के पास भागने की जगह नहीं*
- **Voiceover:** "अंदर पानी भाप बन जाता है। ढक्कन बंद है, तो भाप फँस जाती है और प्रेशर बढ़ता जाता है — जिससे पानी नॉर्मल उबाल से भी ज़्यादा गरम हो जाता है, और खाना आधे वक़्त में पक जाता है।"
- **Flow prompt:** *3D cutaway cross-section of a sealed pressure cooker, 9:16 vertical. Camera dives through the lid into the inside. Water bubbling vigorously, white steam filling the sealed chamber, glowing orange pressure arrows pushing outward against the inner walls. Clean studio-3D look, soft volumetric light.*

## Clip 3 — सीटी (22–34s)
- **स्क्रीन टेक्स्ट:** *ये वज़न ही दरबान है*
- **Voiceover:** "ऊपर रखा ये छोटा-सा वज़न असली दरबान है। जब प्रेशर काफ़ी तेज़ हो जाता है, ये वज़न को ऊपर उठा देता है, भाप एक झटके में बाहर निकलती है — यही है आपकी सीटी — फिर वज़न गिरकर दोबारा सील कर देता है।"
- **Flow prompt:** *Close-up 3D cutaway of the vent pipe and the weighted whistle valve, 9:16 vertical. Pressure builds, the weight lifts a few millimetres, steam jets out sideways in a sharp burst, then the weight drops back and seals. Show the full lift-release-drop cycle once. Dramatic side lighting, crisp detail.*

## Clip 4 — बोनस + लूप (34–45s)
- **स्क्रीन टेक्स्ट:** *हर सीटी = कुकर की साँस*
- **Voiceover:** "और अगर प्रेशर कभी हद से ज़्यादा बढ़ जाए, तो सेफ्टी वाल्व खुलकर उसे बाहर निकाल देता है। तो हर सीटी बस आपके कुकर का साँस छोड़ना है — बिलकुल टाइम पर।"
- **Flow prompt:** *3D cutaway highlighting the rubber safety valve on the cooker lid, 9:16 vertical. A quick glowing callout ring points to it as a small puff releases. Then cut back to the exterior shot from clip 1 — same steel cooker on the stove, same framing, whistle blowing — so the video loops cleanly. Warm photoreal kitchen.*

## Production notes
- Record the Hindi VO separately and lay it over the stitched clips in CapCut — Flow's own audio won't match this narration.
- One action per 10-sec clip — don't cram.
- Word count ~115, sits comfortably in 45 sec with natural pauses.
- **Physics check:** steam trapped under a sealed lid → pressure rises → boiling point rises → faster cooking; the weighted valve lifts only when pressure beats its weight, venting in bursts (the whistle); the safety valve is the backup if it ever goes too far.
